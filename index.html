Core Capabilities

Intelligence at Every Layer

Four AI systems working in concert to predict, detect, and optimize your supply chain — automatically.

DF

Demand Forecasting

XGBoost-powered multi-horizon forecasting trained on historical sales data. Predicts monthly demand with rolling trend analysis across lag features.

RI

Risk Intelligence

Composite risk scoring fusing supplier stability, weather disruption signals, and news-based disruption events. Alerts when thresholds are exceeded.

SC

Scenario Simulator

Digital Twin engine that lets you apply what-if conditions — demand spikes, supplier failures, weather disruptions — and instantly projects the full impact.

DE

AI Decision Engine

Q-learning reinforcement agent trained to recommend optimal actions: reorder stock, switch supplier, reroute shipment, or emergency restock.

System Pipeline

How OptiFlow Thinks

Four intelligent layers process raw signals into actionable decisions automatically.

1

Data Ingestion

Historical sales, supplier inventory, WeatherAPI signals, and India-focused news headlines from NewsData.io are collected and unified.

2

AI Processing

XGBoost forecasts demand. Feature engineering computes composite risk scores. The RL agent evaluates the 9-dimensional state space.

3

Smart Decisions

Q-learning agent selects optimal action with confidence scoring. XAI system generates human-readable explanations for every recommendation.
